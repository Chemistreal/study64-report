# English media archive v1

신뢰도: A 생성 (제작 관리)

This release is the reusable media store for `english.html` and later coding.

- VOA Let's Learn English Level 1: 52 lessons, conversation audio, low-resolution main video, speaking video, pronunciation video, and transcript.
- VOA Let's Learn English Level 2: 30 lessons, low-resolution main video, available conversation audio, and transcript.
- Santa Barbara Corpus of Spoken American English: 60 unchanged TRN and CHAT transcript pairs.
- VOA course guides: four official PDF resources.

VOA-produced Learning English material is public domain with source credit. Santa Barbara Corpus files are CC BY-ND 3.0 US and must remain unchanged. YouTube media is not downloaded or included.

See `media/english/RIGHTS.md` in the repository for the rights ledger and `SHA256SUMS` for archive integrity.
